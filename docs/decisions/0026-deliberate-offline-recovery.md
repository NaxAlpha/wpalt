# Decision 0026 — Separate device snapshots from owner-visible recovery

2026-10-13, accepted within the authorized local-first product direction. [D12 contract](../d12-contract.md).

Use an explicit scoped private-device library of bounded public text/scalar-form snapshots. Do not cache authenticated page HTML or silently retain browsing. Use a separate publication-bound owner policy and visitor-confirmed recovery intent for owner-visible partial responses and a single reminder. Saving on a device or to the private-capability draft store grants neither owner-review nor marketing authority. Original interrupted submission payloads remain immutable until resolved or deliberately abandoned.

New persisted consent state requires native25/portable20 and a reviewed explicit original-source conversion; normal requests never parse an old grammar. OS/browser eviction and disconnected revocation discovery remain transparent limits. No external account is needed for the local outbox and real browser/database proof.
