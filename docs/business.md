# Business and audience platform — M4

The platform keeps forms, responses, audiences, communications and consented engagement in the site's own SQLite or PostgreSQL database. No vendor account or Node runtime is required. Delivery verification is recorded separately in [M4 evidence](evidence/m4-verification.md); the milestone PR is [#8](https://github.com/NaxAlpha/wpalt/pull/8).

## Forms, contributions and recovery

Open **Forms**, create a form and use the visual designer to arrange typed fields, nested reusable groups/repeaters and up to eight steps. Publish deliberately; working changes do not alter the live publication. Conditions refer to earlier fields; numeric sums/products and choice scores are recomputed on the server. Hidden values are discarded. There is no executable expression language. Calculations use finite floating point, not exact currency. Acknowledgments and typed-name signatures preserve the published statement and explicit acceptance; they are not identity certification or cryptographic signing.

Public forms live at `/forms/<id>`. Insert **Published form** in the writing editor or select a published form in the theme composition builder. Same-origin embeds share the surrounding navigation and resize without exchanging response values. Draft previews show an inactive placeholder. Email rendering projects form blocks into absolute links. Public field definitions omit private routing recipients and workflow templates.

The **Workflows** screen configures fixed-recipient canonical messages with presence/equality conditions, mailbox-verified registration and moderated post drafts. Changes to these actions require an administrator. Publishing freezes actions along with the form. Responses, quota admission and actions commit together; a repeated UUID with the original payload returns the original result, while changed payloads conflict. Visitor contributions become literal, escaped draft text and cannot publish themselves. Registration sends an expiring proof, requires a password through the private proof page, then owner approval. Approved accounts have the `subscriber` role and an `/account` page, with no editorial or administrative permissions.

**Save and resume** provides an expiring private server capability and a separate explicit device-storage choice. Server partial drafts validate available values without executing final actions. Capabilities are hashed, form-scoped, revisioned and expire after seven days. Device copies contain responses: use a private device. An already-loaded form can be edited offline; uncertain submissions preserve their original key, version and payload across reload and retry. An accepted response removes the device copy. This is not offline navigation of an uncached site.

Definitions allow 32 top-level fields, shared schema depth/expanded-node budgets, and eight ordered steps. Requests are capped at 128 KiB. Each form defaults to 10,000 accepted entries, configurable to 1,000,000. Site-wide record/byte limits also apply. A stale publication cannot accept a new response; accepted retries remain available even at capacity.

## Private files and response management

Protected uploads accept UTF-8 text, PDF signature checks and decoded PNG/JPEG/WebP images re-encoded as PNG. Each file is limited to the smaller of `max_upload_bytes` and 2 MiB; image decoding is limited to 4,096 pixels and 64 MiB allocation. PDFs are not malware scanned or disarmed. UTF-8 filenames are transported explicitly. Files use private UUID `.dat` names and 0600 permissions, separate from public media. Random capabilities expire after 24 hours and attach once to the matching form/publication/field. Each form is capped at 1,000 files / 64 MiB, in addition to global limits.

Responses support FTS5/GIN token search, date/ID keyset pages of 40, versioned private notes and assignment to active editors. CSV exports use ID pages of 500 with quoting and spreadsheet-formula guards. Authenticated downloads verify ownership and integrity. Accepted responses/files are retained; audience deletion does not remove independent response records or historical backups. Pending uploads and server drafts expire through bounded maintenance. Filesystem interruption may leave orphan private files; independent backups and filesystem administration remain necessary.

## Audience and communications

Create purpose-specific lists with a policy version. A checked consent field creates a pending membership and one confirmation message transactionally; repeated submissions cannot flood a still-valid proof. GET proof pages have no consent side effects. Deliberate confirmation is required before campaigns, and withdrawal cannot be silently undone by anonymous requests. Contacts have bounded company/source/score attributes; segments combine exact company/source and minimum score conditions. Contact detail supports suppression, bounded export and deliberate deletion. Deletion removes audience history and associated queue/outbox data; an owner-keyed address digest prevents anonymous recreation. Already accepted SMTP messages cannot be recalled.

Compose campaigns with the shared structured editor. Save, schedule using the browser's local date/time control, send now, or enable a reusable **subscriber confirmation** trigger. Triggered templates use current attributes/segment and deduplicate a confirmation transition. They do not retroactively send to previously confirmed subscribers. Scheduled expansion uses contact-ID keysets, a confirmation cutoff and batches of 50. Consent is checked during expansion and again immediately before transport.

`[mail]` defaults to enabled, sender `site@localhost.test`, five attempts, batch size 64 and no SMTP connections. The default produces private deterministic `.eml` files in `data_dir/outbox`; this is spool success, not internet delivery. Configure at most four `[[mail.smtp]]` routes with `host`, `port`, `tls`, `username`, `password`. `starttls` and `tls` use bundled WebPKI roots; plaintext `local` requires an explicit loopback IP. File batches are capped at 64 and network batches at eight per scheduler tick. Typed configuration output redacts database and SMTP credentials.

Mail exposes queued, leased, retry, spooled, sent, uncertain, dead and cancelled states with bounded attempt history. Database leases coordinate independent workers. Expired leases or ambiguous network acceptance become **uncertain**, never automatic replay. Known negative/setup failures can retry or use configured fallback; deliberate resend requires acknowledgment of duplicates. No exactly-once internet delivery, inbox reputation, DKIM signing or RFC8058 one-click compliance is claimed. Use an owner-operated MTA for real delivery.

## Consented engagement and local offers

Collection starts **off**. Enable it in **Engagement** and state its purpose. Visitors can explicitly allow analytics, separately opt into masked recording, decline, or withdraw. Global Privacy Control and configured Do Not Track deny collection. Purpose/catalog/promotion changes require renewed consent. Withdrawal erases the current visitor's session, events, impressions and receipts; inventory already issued remains consumed.

Pageviews and declared events accept only registered names, published public paths and declared category values. Custom dimensions are bounded enums, not free-text identifiers. Accepted responses and offer claims create server-confirmed conversion events; visitors cannot forge them. Reports include bounded event/path/category totals and the pageview → accepted response → offer claim funnel. Counts are cookie sessions, not identified people; timestamps have one-second resolution. The current funnel is intentionally this defined workflow, not an arbitrary visual funnel designer.

Recording stores only quantized rectangles, viewport, scroll, elapsed time and optional click coordinates. It never records DOM text, field values, links, credentials or screenshots. Owner playback and click heatmaps draw a bounded wireframe, not a DOM replay. Limits are 500 events / 60 frames per session and 200 rectangles per frame, plus configurable global bounds and retention.

**Local offers** combines canonical A/B messages, stable consented-session allocation, public paths, device/referrer categories, schedule and impression caps. Popups use native accessible dialogs and keyboard dismissal. Optional weighted reward draws allocate stock atomically and produce a duplicate-safe receipt once per promotion/session. Variant reports include impressions, retained sessions and claims. Clearing cookies creates a new session; allocation is not person verification. Purchase events, checkout and redemption belong to M6. Statistical significance and legal promotion rules are not inferred from small samples.

## Limits, disabling and recovery

See `wpalt.example.toml` for `[business_limits]`, `[mail]` and `[engagement]`. Admission uses persistent conditional counters shared by all pools; rejected operations roll back their actions. Pending proofs, staged files, drafts and old terminal mail are cleaned in batches of 50. Uncertain mail is retained for owner decisions; accepted entries are not automatically erased. Backpressure is explicit when configured capacity is reached.

`business_enabled = false` removes business routes/assets and skips business jobs. `engagement.enabled = false` removes analytics/offer routes/assets and skips engagement retention; other enabled business work remains available. Turning collection off in administration omits public capture assets; previously stored data can still expire. Collection settings are not a blanket privacy-law certification.

Schema/backup format 7 covers business state, private files, capabilities, quota counters and engagement geometry. Fresh restore validates semantic relationships and counters, retains immutable form meanings, marks outstanding network leases uncertain and reconstructs local spools. A one-time upgrade expands the user-role constraint and creates current tables/indexes; meaningful M3.5 data is preserved. Restore old backups with their matching older binary before upgrading the recovered database. Take an independent backup first; no parallel legacy runtime is retained.

Debug telemetry records request/SQL timings, IDs, outcomes and error codes. It omits responses, recipients, passwords, capabilities, message bodies and credentials. Capability screens are `no-store`, `noindex` and `no-referrer`. Guidance is versioned in `evidence/feature-guidance.json` and checked before delivery; deployed installations do not download executable policy.
