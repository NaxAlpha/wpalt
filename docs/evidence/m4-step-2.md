PR #8 remains draft. This checkpoint does not declare M4 complete or reduce its contract.

Working slices now connect form admission to purpose/version-specific pending audience membership and durable confirmation mail in one transaction. GET is review only; deliberate recipient POST confirms or withdraws. Structured campaigns reuse the post editor/document grammar, expand at most 50 candidates per transaction, apply typed bounded segments and deduplicate recipient jobs. Local deterministic file spooling is default; owner-selected SMTP routes expose accepted, retry, dead and uncertain outcomes. Lost receipts and expired leases are not blindly replayed. Manual resend acknowledges duplicate risk. Contact suppression/deletion cancels or removes audience mail and retains an owner-keyed address guard that serializes anonymous recreation with deletion.

Forms also support choice/score/long-text/email presentation, typed-name statement acknowledgment, nested objects/repeaters, expiring revisioned server drafts and opt-in bounded device recovery. Private files have form/version/field-scoped single-entry upload capabilities, capped admission, generated paths, image re-encoding and owner-only downloads. Response search uses FTS5 or GIN, keyset paging, versioned private follow-up and bounded exports. Backup-v6 includes meaningful new state and private bytes; restore conservatively recovers leases and local spool reconstruction.
