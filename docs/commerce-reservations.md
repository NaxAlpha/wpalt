# Local commerce and reservations

M6 delivers the defined local store and reservation workflows in [PR #12](https://github.com/NaxAlpha/wpalt/pull/12). See [the contract](m6-contract.md) and [verification](evidence/m6-verification.md) for delivery gates and evidence. Merge and production/provider-account certification are separate decisions.

## Start a store

Initialize and run wpalt as described in [operations](operations.md). An administrator opens **Commerce** (`/admin/shop`), creates a product, adds at least one active variant and publishes it. Customers use `/shop`, the cart, private orders and subscriptions with their existing accounts. The optional offline demo is `wpalt shop seed-demo` while the server is stopped: four synthetic physical/digital/membership/booking products and a group appointment. It never simulates a bank transfer or card charge.

Each product supports at most 100 variants. Catalog/order/calendar pages use bounded cursors; pickers for resources, booking variants, discounts and eligible plans show at most 100 records, with stable-ID CLI/domain operations for larger operator configurations. This is a documented native-picker limitation, not unlimited interface coverage.

Physical variants have a total stock count, including already sold and held units. Digital, membership and booking variants use `-1` for inventory; bookings have separate slot capacity. Enter human decimal prices in the web interface, or integer minor units in CLI JSON. USD/EUR/GBP use cents; JPY uses whole yen. Configured member pricing requires the current matching entitlement. Prices/tax/shipping/discount rules are recalculated at checkout and snapshotted in the order. Changed terms reject an old quote rather than silently charge a new amount. Product type, access key and protected file cannot change underneath existing purchases: create another product instead.

A digital product can reference an existing private media item. Its shared member access rule must match the product entitlement and cannot weaken a course/group policy. Purchasing creates access through the existing protected-media delivery path. Membership products specify the access key of the course/content policy they unlock. A fixed-price recurring membership variant specifies day/week/month/year; recurring carts contain one variant, quantity one, without member-price/coupon adjustments. One-time access may be permanent or a configured number of seconds.

## Checkout and recorded payments

Signed-in carts persist across restart, hold at most 20 lines and allow quantities 1–100 (digital/membership one). The server controls prices, eligible discounts, tax and stock. An order reserves finite stock and slot capacity in the same transaction as its immutable lines. Replayed checkout keys return the same order; changed payloads conflict. The default unpaid hold expires after 900 seconds. Free offline orders settle at zero without collecting money. An unavailable item can be removed from the cart even after it is unpublished.

Offline orders remain **awaiting** until an administrator records money actually received, with a real bank/cash/manual receipt reference. Payment and fulfillment are distinct. Fulfill physical purchases/reservations separately; digital/membership access is fulfilled by confirmed settlement. Do not use the recorded-payment action to invent money. Customers see only their own receipts and may cancel an unpaid order or request cancellation of a paid one. A paid cancellation request does not automatically reverse money or free stock.

A store has one currency, configured before initialization. Reopening with another currency fails. Merchant-set tax basis points and flat physical shipping are local rules: the order preserves the calculation and rounding. Tax uses the discounted eligible subtotal and the declared shipping-tax choice. These records can be printed but are not jurisdiction-certified tax invoices. Tax datasets/filing, carrier rates/labels and external-calendar synchronization are optional external boundaries.

Refund authorization reserves the remaining paid balance, including outstanding refunds. Recording actual completion changes financial state. Partial refunds do not restore quantities/access; a full refund releases booking capacity and revokes only the purchase's own grants. Physical restocking is an explicit final full-refund choice with no other outstanding refund. A refund after an affiliate payout can leave a negative outstanding balance; wpalt never fabricates a clawback.

## Reservations and communications

Resources may have an assigned active administrator/editor account. Create explicit UTC start/end slots and group capacity. Manage resources can rename, pause or reassign resources; stale edits and assignments that overlap another staff booking are rejected. New allocations recheck the current assigned staff role. Slots cannot overlap for the same resource or assigned staff, including occupied inactive slots. A slot is at most a day long and initially at most a year ahead. The atomic hold competes for the actual last slot, not merely a cached calendar display. A reservation uses the booking variant price and appears in the customer's order.

Confirmed orders generate durable local service notifications; bookings have confirmation and a reminder 24 hours before their start when booked more than a day ahead. Cancellation withdraws pending/retry confirmations/reminders and adds a cancellation notice. Mail already sent or in-flight cannot be recalled. The shared outbox can spool `.eml` locally without an SMTP provider, or use configured SMTP. Disabled mail retains pending notifications; it does not report delivery. Checkout creates a shared contact without marketing consent or list subscription.

## Subscription rules

Paid access is bounded by the settled billing period. Offline renewal invoices are generated up to three days before the period ends. At expiry, an unpaid subscription becomes **past_due** and old access expires. The next period remains collectible until that period's end; no scheduler fabricates collection. An invoice is created once per period, with the original agreed tax rules and a snapshotted price. Extended missed periods cancel instead of creating an unbounded backlog.

Offline plan changes require the same access key and billing interval. An upgrade creates an unpaid prorated invoice for the remaining period; the new plan takes effect only after actual payment. A downgrade records its accepted price for the next paid period. Resolve an outstanding upgrade/renewal before changing again. Cancelling stops outstanding local invoices/future billing and preserves the currently paid period. Refunding a paid order does not revoke unrelated owner grants or another paid order's grants.

## Optional hosted Stripe

Set `commerce.stripe.enabled=true`, an HTTPS `base_url`, `WPALT_STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` and `WPALT_STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`. Secrets are redacted from `wpalt config` and never rendered in pages. Configure a Stripe webhook at `POST /commerce/stripe/webhook` with **API version 2026-09-30.endive** and the checkout completion/async success/failure/expiry, invoice paid/payment failed, subscription update/delete and refund create/update events. Redirects to the local receipt are navigation only.

The connector posts authoritative amounts to hosted Checkout. No card entry occurs in wpalt. Raw-body HMAC verification accepts a bounded timestamp window before any event is stored. Event IDs/payload digests and provider idempotency keys prevent replay effects. The worker retrieves canonical sessions, PaymentIntents, invoices/subscriptions and refunds through authenticated HTTPS without redirects. Exact currency, amount, mapping and paid state are required before granting access. Unknown/out-of-order mappings remain pending for safe retry. Expired/cancelled holds receiving late verified money become **needs_refund**, never overbooked or newly entitled. Hosted holds last at least 1800 seconds to meet Checkout's expiry rule.

Hosted recurring collection supports one fixed-price subscription item and one exact uncredited invoice payment. Split payments, account credits, usage billing, trials, external plan edits and heterogeneous subscription items require explicit future integration work/operator reconciliation. Local offline proration is implemented; hosted plan changes are not claimed. Hosted cancellation requests period-end cancellation for an established paid period; a not-yet-mapped pending purchase can be cancelled locally, and any later verified settlement is retained for refund without granting access. A full subscription refund or late recurring payment persists a separate provider billing-stop job; failed transport retries after 30 seconds and the obligation survives fresh recovery. Local access state does not assert that an unavailable provider has stopped billing. Refunds retain a pending record on uncertain transport; retry uses the same provider idempotency key. Provider account requirements/minimum charges remain provider rules. Deterministic local HTTPS fixtures verify the connector; no live/sandbox account certification is implied without separate evidence.

## Configuration, CLI and recovery

`wpalt.example.toml` documents `commerce.enabled`, currency, holds, row quota, referral basis points and optional provider transport. `WPALT_COMMERCE_ENABLED` is an environment override. Disabled commerce has no routes/navigation/background work; existing data remains in owner recovery. One owning process per data directory remains the supported M1–M6 topology for either SQLite or PostgreSQL; independent multi-server coordination is M9.

`wpalt shop --help` lists bounded JSON product/variant/discount/slot imports, resources, store rules, actual payments, authorized/refund completion, fulfillment, maintenance, a redacted operational report and the synthetic demo. CLI uses the same domain validation under the existing process lock; stop the server before offline mutations. Use `--id` and `--version` for optimistic edits. JSON input types are `ProductInput`, `VariantInput`, `DiscountInput`, `SlotInput`, `RefundInput` in `src/commerce`; order totals are never CLI/client authority.

Back up and stop the merged M5 runtime before upgrade. Schema 8 migrates additively to schema 9; rename any older content using the newly reserved `shop`/`commerce` slug with the old runtime first (a conflict rejects and rolls back the upgrade); currency is initialized from the effective configuration. Unpublished M6 previews may reset their synthetic databases rather than retain compatibility shims. Current backups use `wpalt-backup-v8`/schema 9 and include financial snapshots, subscriptions, allocations, provider events, service notifications, reward redemption and payout/history records alongside earlier features. Restore to a fresh target with the same currency. Legacy schema-8 archives require the matching M5 runtime, then normal upgrade and re-export; no old-archive parser remains in M6. Derived quota counters are rebuilt from restored inserts. Backups contain private customer and provider-event data: retain them securely and independently.

The backup checksum detects accidental changes, not a malicious author. Typed allowlists, cross-record financial/access/capacity checks and database constraints are the recovery trust boundary. Validation precedes file writes. Actual offline payments, provider transmission ambiguity and in-flight mail remain explicit operational facts after restart.

## Reproduce resource observations

`python3 scripts/benchmark.py --binary target/release/wpalt --posts 1000 --requests 100 --composed --discovery --commerce --output work/m6-runtime.json` creates a disposable publishing/store fixture, records the executable hash/bytes, RSS after load, site disk bytes and bounded HTTP latency samples. Add `--debug` to measure diagnostic logging separately; logs are excluded from the persistent-site byte count and reported separately. These are localhost application observations, not browser render timing or production capacity. Populated cart/private-order query plans and latencies are exercised by `commerce_populated_catalog_cart_and_order_history_remain_bounded_and_indexed` on both supported engines.
